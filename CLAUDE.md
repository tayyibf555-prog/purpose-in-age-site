Context for the Purpose in Age website build. Read this before writing code.

## What the business is

Purpose in Age is a personal brand and community business built around one man: Scott D Brown, 63, based in San Francisco with a second office in Sonoma County, California.

It is not a service business, not e-commerce, and not SaaS. The product is Scott's credibility, and everything else hangs off it. The correct mental model is an author platform (Daily Stoic, Mark Manson, Modern Elder Academy), not a company website.

The market: active ageing, skewed male, roughly 55 to 80. Specifically the purpose and identity slice of that market, not health, not financial planning. The thesis is that a man's identity collapses when his career ends, and that this rebuilds it.

Origin: Scott runs a local chapter of ROMEO (Retired Old Men Eating Out), an informal, dues-free network of retired men who meet for meals. That table is where the whole idea came from and it is the emotional centre of the brand.

What Scott is: a career marketer, thirty years, retired at 61. He is not a doctor, therapist, gerontologist or financial adviser. The site must never imply otherwise.

## What it sells

Ordered by priority for this build:

1. **Purpose in Age Club**, a paid membership community. Does not exist yet. Pre-launch, so the site captures a waitlist rather than selling. This is the primary conversion goal.
2. **The book**, *A Man's Purpose Never Retires. It Evolves.* Published via Leaders Press, a hybrid publisher, meaning Scott paid to publish. The book is a credibility instrument, not a revenue line. Chapter one is the lead magnet.
3. **Speaking and keynotes**, real, and possibly the highest-margin current activity. Needs a proper page.
4. **Author services ("Purpose on the Page")**, planned, not built. Different buyer. Keep off the homepage.

## The single job of the site

Turn a visitor into a name on the email list.

Every section either does that, or supports the two things that make it possible: believing in Scott, and understanding what the Club is. If a section cannot answer "how does this get someone onto the list", it does not ship.

## Who reads it

A 66-year-old man, twelve to twenty-four months out of a long career, on an iPad or desktop, in daylight, mildly sceptical, does not think of himself as old, does not want to be sold to.

This is a hard constraint on the build, not a persona exercise:

- Body text 20px minimum. Never grey body text. Full contrast.
- Eyebrow and caption text 14px minimum, never 10px.
- Buttons minimum 48px tall, solid fills, verb-first labels. No ghost buttons anywhere.
- No hover-dependent content. Nothing hidden behind an interaction he has to discover.
- Desktop navigation stays visible. No hamburger above 768px.
- No text sitting directly on detailed photography without a scrim or a solid panel behind it.
- WCAG AA minimum, and aim past it.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Design system

**Superseded.** The stone / paper / ink / oxblood slab system originally specified here was replaced at the client's direction: the redesign follows their own reference set rather than a system written up front.

The client chose the **Orix Creative "Building Beyond"** reference as the anchor, from a set that also included Quad, EverGreen, Stayli and Flowform. That means a deep clay ground carrying the whole page, bone panels floating on it with a pill notched into a corner, a heavy grotesque broken by one Didone italic phrase, and full-bleed photography.

**`DESIGN.md` is the authoritative spec.** Read it before touching anything visual. Do not reinstate the slab system.

What carried over unchanged, because it comes from the reader being 55 to 80 rather than from any visual system:

- Body 20px minimum, labels 15px minimum, never grey body text.
- Buttons 52px minimum, solid fills, verb-first labels, no ghost buttons.
- No hover-dependent content. Desktop nav always visible, no hamburger above 768px.
- No text on unmediated photography.
- WCAG AA minimum, and the shipped palette clears AAA on all but one pairing.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.

## Stack

Next.js on Vercel. Sanity for content. n8n for the content pipeline. Resend or ConvertKit for email.

The Club will need a members area within a year, so the codebase should be able to absorb that rather than depending on a third-party embed.

## Content pipeline

Scott already produces good material (LinkedIn posts several times a week, podcast appearances, curated links) and none of it reaches his site. The pipeline ingests that, drafts a post, and holds it as an unpublished draft for Scott to review before it goes live.

The human review step is a required deliverable, not an implementation detail. The current site is unreviewed AI content stuffed with repeated keyword phrases, and it is unreadable. Do not build an auto-publish path.

## Copy rules

- No em dashes. Use commas, full stops, or rephrase.
- Sentence case. Never Title Case, never all caps except tracked eyebrow labels.
- Calm, grounded, anti-hype. Plain verbs. No "unlock", "empower", "seamless", "thriving", "vitality", "expert lifestyle tips". Those last three are the exact phrases stuffed through the current site.
- First person for anything in Scott's voice. His LinkedIn writing is the reference for tone and is markedly better than anything on his current site.
- Specific beats clever. "I lift at a Planet Fitness in Rohnert Park on Mondays and Thursdays" is worth more than any adjective.
- US English, since the audience is American.

## Things deliberately not on the site

Removed from the current build: the brand endorsement wall (AG1, AARP, Lululemon, Charles Schwab, Planet Fitness, Apple, Road Scholar and others), Boardsi, HSP Brands, the Social Security explainer, the San Francisco photo montage, "Heavy Hitting Friends", merch and wine links, third-party bios, and event travel logistics.

Most of that is network reciprocity rather than visitor value. If any of it has to stay, it goes on a single quiet "Friends of Purpose in Age" page linked from the footer.

The endorsement wall specifically must not be recreated. Those links are not affiliate-tracked and the relationships appear to be aspirational, which is a credibility exposure rather than a monetisation route.

## Assumptions not yet confirmed by the client

Treat these as working hypotheses, not facts. Do not write copy that depends on them.

- Whether Scott has an existing email list, and its size.
- The Club's launch date, price, platform and weekly format.
- The book's publication date. The title also appears inconsistently in his own materials, sometimes "Never Retires", sometimes "Doesn't Retire". Confirm before setting it in type.
- Whether any brand relationship is actually paid.
- Details of Scott's personal story. Any biographical copy currently in the mockups is indicative placeholder written to demonstrate tone, and must be replaced with his own words.
- Testimonial names and quotes in the mockups are invented placeholders.

## Correction: Scott has not retired

The original brief described Scott as having "retired at 61", and the first draft of this site built its central narrative on that. **It is false**, and it was caught by scraping his LinkedIn (`/in/sdb5/`).

His profile lists 41 roles, including several that are current:

- Founder, SDB05 Digital Strategies LLC, from July 2026
- Digital Strategy and Analytics Leader, Pure Mission Entertainment, from July 2026
- Book Author, Purpose in Age, from March 2026
- Chapter Founder, Sonoma County ROMEO Club, from January 2026
- Brand Ambassador, HSP Brands, from July 2025
- eBook Author, Bookboon, from March 2021

He is actively working. A site claiming he retired, next to a public profile with 4,412 followers saying otherwise, is a ten second credibility failure. It also contradicted his own book title, *A Man's Purpose Never Retires. It Evolves.*

**The real arc is stronger and is now what the site tells.** After roughly twenty years in sales and network services (InfoSend 1996, Medical Manager, WebMD and Emdeon, Sage), he moved in 2019 into frontline social services: assistant program manager at Lutheran Social Services, then **case manager II at US Vets**, then two years of housing work with Lift Up and Harbor Interfaith. Since then: XR Metaverse Studios founded and closed, pro bono fractional CMO for the Golden Gate Center for Spiritual Living, a nonprofit board seat at Sonoma CAN, the ROMEO chapter and the book.

That is a purpose that evolved rather than stopped, which is the thesis the whole business rests on.

`timeline` in `src/content/site.ts` holds the dated spine. Every entry maps to a role on that profile.

**Two real recommendations** are in `recommendations`, from Philip Gwartney (2013) and Mary Murray Shelton, Senior Minister at the Golden Gate Center for Spiritual Living (2024). Both need written permission before launch.

**On tone.** His LinkedIn headline reads "the KING of Marketing Books | Visionary | Guru of Marketing and Media" and his summary is a list of superlatives. Take the facts, leave the register. That voice is the same one that made the current website unreadable, and it would undo the "I am a marketer, not a therapist" positioning the rebuild depends on.

## Known issues on the existing site, for reference

Useful context for what we are replacing and why. Current site is GoDaddy Website Builder.

- Card grid partly unfilled: several headings repeat three or four times, and card bodies contain content belonging to other cards.
- Blog posts do not render. Requesting a post URL returns the homepage, so everything Scott has written is effectively invisible.

- Several dead CTAs, including shop links pointing at Printful's homepage.
- Heavy keyword stuffing throughout.
- Hero is a stock clip of a man surfing, which sets an aspiration most of the audience will read as "not for me".

## Build conventions

- All copy awaiting Scott's input lives in `src/content/site.ts`, and every unconfirmed string carries a `PLACEHOLDER` tag so it can be found and replaced in one pass. Run `npm run placeholders` to list them.
- No invented testimonials, no invented statistics, no press logos we do not have. If a section needs social proof we do not have, the section does not ship.
- Motion is GSAP with ScrollTrigger, driven through `useGSAP` so it reverts on route change. See the motion section of `DESIGN.md` for the two escape hatches and the `clearProps` trap.
- Photography is currently AI generated and is **placeholder**. Every image must be replaced with real photography before launch, and the speaking image in particular must not be read as a portrait of Scott.
- Add `?nomotion` to any URL to render the settled layout with no animation, for reviewing spacing and type.
