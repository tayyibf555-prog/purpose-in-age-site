# PRODUCT.md

## Register

**brand**

Design is the product. This is an author platform, not an application. Every surface exists to make one man credible and to move a visitor onto an email list.

## Product purpose

Purpose in Age is the personal brand of Scott D Brown, 63, a career marketer of thirty years who retired at 61 and found the exit harder than he expected. He runs a local chapter of ROMEO (Retired Old Men Eating Out), an informal, dues free network of retired men who meet for meals. That table is the origin of the business and the emotional centre of the brand.

The thesis: a man's identity collapses when his career ends. Purpose in Age rebuilds it.

The site has one job. Turn a visitor into a name on the email list. Any section that cannot answer "how does this get someone onto the list" does not ship.

What it sells, in build priority order:

1. Purpose in Age Club, a paid membership. Pre launch, so the site captures a waitlist.
2. The book, *A Man's Purpose Never Retires. It Evolves.* A credibility instrument, not a revenue line. Chapter one is the lead magnet.
3. Speaking and keynotes. Real, and probably the highest margin activity today.
4. Author services, planned, not built, different buyer, kept off the homepage.

## Users

One reader. A 66 year old man, twelve to twenty four months out of a long career. Reading on an iPad or a desktop, in daylight. Mildly sceptical. Does not think of himself as old. Does not want to be sold to.

He is not browsing for inspiration. He is quietly trying to work out whether the next twenty years have a shape. He will leave immediately if the site talks to him as a patient, a demographic, or a decline to be managed.

This produces hard build constraints, not a persona exercise:

- Body text 20px minimum. Never grey body text. Full contrast.
- Eyebrow and caption text 14px minimum, never 10px.
- Buttons 48px tall minimum, solid fills, verb first labels. No ghost buttons anywhere.
- No hover dependent content. Nothing hidden behind an interaction he has to discover.
- Desktop navigation stays visible. No hamburger above 768px.
- No text sitting directly on detailed photography without a scrim or a solid panel behind it.
- WCAG AA minimum, and aim past it.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Brand voice

Calm, grounded, anti hype. Plain verbs. First person for anything in Scott's voice.

Specific beats clever. "I lift at a Planet Fitness in Rohnert Park on Mondays and Thursdays" is worth more than any adjective on the page.

Banned words: unlock, empower, seamless, thriving, vitality, expert lifestyle tips. The last three are the exact phrases stuffed through the current site.

No em dashes. Sentence case. Never Title Case, never all caps except tracked eyebrow labels. US English.

Three brand voice words, as physical objects: **a heavy diner mug, a well kept tool, a table with the plates cleared.**

## Anti references

- **Retirement industry visual language.** Soft focus couples on beaches, sailboats, pastel blues and sage greens, stock photography of silver haired people laughing at salad. This is the single strongest thing to avoid. It signals decline management.
- **Wellness and longevity brands.** Clinical whites, teals, biometrics, supplement aesthetics. Purpose in Age is not a health product.
- **Financial services.** Navy and gold, columns, handshakes, trust badges.
- **Hustle and coaching culture.** High contrast black and yellow, countdown timers, "transform your life", testimonial walls, arrows pointing at buttons.
- **The current site.** GoDaddy Website Builder, roughly twenty sections on one page, keyword stuffing, an endorsement wall of brands with no commercial relationship, a hero video of a man surfing that reads to most of the audience as "not for me".
- **Editorial magazine pastiche.** Display serif italic, drop caps, ruled three column metadata. Saturated, and the wrong register for this brief. The design system bans serifs outright.

## Strategic principles

1. **Credibility is the product.** Scott is a marketer, not a doctor, therapist, gerontologist or financial adviser. The site must never imply otherwise. Nothing invented: no fabricated testimonials, no invented statistics, no press logos we do not have.
2. **Subtraction is the work.** The current site has roughly twenty sections on one page. The redesign is eight routes. Resist adding.
3. **The table is the centre.** ROMEO, men eating together, is the truest asset the brand has. Lead with it rather than with abstractions about purpose.
4. **Oxblood carries the brand.** It reads as appetite and vigour rather than decline, and nobody in this category uses it. Commit to it, do not hedge it with neutrals.
5. **Dignity over aspiration.** Show men who look like the reader, doing ordinary specific things, not an idealised retirement he will read as belonging to someone else.
6. **Every placeholder is tagged.** Scott's real biography, the Club's price and format, and the book's publication date are all unconfirmed. Copy must not depend on them, and placeholders must be findable in one pass.
